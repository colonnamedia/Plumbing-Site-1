# FireWorks project handoff — Blue Harbor Plumbing

- Template: Blue Harbor Plumbing; primary industry plumbing; adaptable to drain/sewer, water heater, residential or commercial plumbing after client scope verification.
- Repository: https://github.com/colonnamedia/Plumbing-Site-1
- Preview: https://plumbing-site-1.vercel.app/
- Pages: Home, Services, Drain Cleaning, Leak Repair, Water Heaters, Service Areas, Request Service. Reusable service detail, trust, coverage, urgent guidance, and intake sections.
- Primary CTA: request service. Demo form fields: name, email, phone, service ZIP, type, urgency, contact preference, problem description. It validates locally and never sends/stores/monitors inquiries.
- Build recommendation: $1,499 for seven pages and structured intake. Hosting separately $499/year primary or $59.99/month. Domain $20/year.
- Optional add-ons: provider-selected email/CRM delivery and spam/privacy setup, dispatch or scheduling, call tracking, photo intake; quote each after scope and credentials, never assume a provider.
- Client inputs: identity, logo, actual phone, address, business hours, verified service boundaries, credentials and license numbers, real reviews and image permission, emergency availability, services and warranties, form destination/privacy terms.
- SEO scaffold: unique titles/descriptions/URLs, internal links, semantic headings, page schema, sitemap; demo noindex until verified customization. No city-name duplicate pages or ranking promise.
- Design variants later: technician photography with permission; copper blueprint; water texture. Current hero is code-native pipe artwork, not a fake work photo.

## Launch checks

Run `npm run check`, `npm run build`; verify navigation, mobile menu, all seven pages, form validation and no-send status, Vercel production, and storefront card. Do not turn on real form delivery or emergency claims without client approval and provider credentials.
